🌍 ABOUT EARTH FORWARD

Making Waste Decisions Simpler

Earth Forward is an AI-powered waste analysis tool designed to help people understand what their waste means for the environment and what they can do with it.

🤖

AI-Powered Analysis

Earth Forward analyzes waste items and provides information about disposal, environmental impact, and practical eco-friendly actions.

🌱

Understand Your Eco Score

Every analyzed item receives an Eco Score based on recyclability, compostability/biodegradibility, environmental risk, disposal difficulty, and special handling.

♻️

Find Better Disposal Options

Earth Forward can help you find nearby recycling and waste facilities so you can make more informed disposal decisions.

🌎

Our Goal

Make responsible waste disposal easier to understand, easier to access, and easier to act on.
